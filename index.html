Under the Hood

How Perde catches you

A multi-layered AI pipeline that runs entirely on your phone. No internet needed.

👁️

App Watcher

Monitors which app is in the foreground. Only activates screen capture when a relevant app is open — not during banking, maps, or calls. Saves battery by sleeping when it's not needed.

📸

Screen Scanner

Captures your screen at 1 frame per second at quarter resolution. Frame differencing detects when the screen hasn't changed and skips analysis — cutting AI work by ~90%.

🧠

AI Classifier

A MobileNetV2 neural network classifies each frame into 5 categories: drawings, hentai, neutral, porn, sexy. Runs entirely on your phone — quantized to ~4 MB, GPU-accelerated when available.

⚖️

Sustained Viewing Detection

This is the key. One NSFW frame doesn't trigger a block — you might just be scrolling. Perde waits for 5 out of 8 consecutive frames to show explicit content. That's 5+ seconds of deliberate viewing. That's when it knows.

🛡️

Intervention

Full-screen overlay. Motivational message from your chosen faith or secular text. Home button pressed. The mindless spiral is interrupted. You're back to reality with a reminder of why you installed this.

Smart enough to not ruin your day

Perde doesn't panic at a bikini photo in your feed. Five layers make sure only sustained explicit viewing triggers a block:

1
Class weighting — "sexy" (beach, sports, fashion) is weighted at 0.35×. Only explicitly pornographic content scores high.
2
Score smoothing — Exponential moving average prevents single-frame spikes from affecting the score.
3
Window voting (5 of 8) — 5 out of 8 consecutive frames must score above threshold. Scrolling past a thumbnail won't trigger it.
4
Hysteresis — Block activates at 0.68, releases at 0.40. Prevents the block screen from flickering.
5
Cooldown — After a block clears, 4-second grace period before re-triggering. No annoying loops.
